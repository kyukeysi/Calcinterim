# Calcinterim

An air-drawn mathematical spellcasting game made with Python, OpenCV, MediaPipe, and SymPy.

The player uses their webcam and hand gestures to draw mathematical symbols and expressions in the air using a glowing magical trail.

## Concept

```text
Webcam
   ↓
MediaPipe hand tracking
   ↓
Index finger drawing
   ↓
Magical spell trail
   ↓
Stroke recognition
   ↓
Mathematical expression
   ↓
SymPy
   ↓
Target comparison
